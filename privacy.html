We use cookies and similar technologies to ensure our website works properly and to understand how it is used.

Learn more about the cookies we use and your choices.⌄

Cookies are small files or identifiers stored on your browser or device that allow a website to recognise certain information about your visit. We may use the following types of cookies:

Essential cookies

These are required for core website functions such as:

  • maintaining your shopping bag;
  • remembering your session;
  • providing secure checkout;
  • protecting the website against fraud; and
  • maintaining account functionality.
Preference cookies

These may remember choices such as language, region or other website preferences.

Analytics cookies

Where enabled, these help us understand how visitors use our website so that we can monitor performance and improve the user experience.

Where required or appropriate, you will be given choices concerning non-essential cookies. You may also be able to restrict or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functions.

For full details of the cookies we use, see our Cookie Policy.
