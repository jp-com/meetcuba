1.  What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files or identifiers that are stored on your browser or device when you visit a website.

Learn more about what cookies do.⌄

They allow websites to recognise your browser or device and may be used to:

  • keep a shopping bag active;
  • maintain a secure session;
  • remember preferences;
  • enable account functionality;
  • process checkout;
  • prevent fraud;
  • understand how a website is being used; and
  • where permitted, provide measurement or other optional functionality.

Some cookies expire when you close your browser, while others may remain on your device for a defined period.

2.  Are Cookies Personal Data?

Cookies do not always contain personal data.

However, information collected through cookies or similar technologies may constitute personal data where it can reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, to an identifiable individual.

Where information collected through cookies constitutes personal data, we handle it in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable Hong Kong data-protection requirements.

Learn more about why we use cookies.⌄

To remember products placed in your shopping bag, maintain your session and facilitate checkout.

To help protect our website, customers and transactions against fraud, abuse and unauthorised access.

To remember choices such as language, region or other website settings where applicable.

Other optional purposes

Where permitted and enabled, cookies may be used for additional measurement, personalisation or other purposes described to you when those cookies are offered.

4.  Types of Cookies We Use

Our website may use several categories of cookies.

Learn more about the categories of cookies we use.⌄
Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the website to function properly.

They may be used for:

  • shopping-bag functionality;
  • secure checkout;
  • account authentication;
  • fraud prevention;
  • website security;
  • session management;
  • load balancing; and
  • remembering privacy choices.

Because these cookies are necessary to provide functionality you request, they generally cannot be disabled through our cookie-preference controls.

You may still be able to block them through your browser, but doing so may cause parts of the website to stop working correctly.

Functional Cookies

Functional cookies allow the website to remember choices you make and provide enhanced functionality.

These may include:

  • language preference;
  • location or region;
  • display preferences; and
  • previously selected website settings.

If you do not allow these cookies, certain convenience features may not operate as intended.

Analytics and Performance Cookies

Where enabled, analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website.

They may collect information such as:

  • pages viewed;
  • navigation paths;
  • time spent on pages;
  • device type;
  • browser type;
  • approximate technical location information;
  • referring website; and
  • website errors or performance information.

We use this information to understand general website usage and improve our services.

Where analytics information can identify or be linked to an individual, it will be handled as personal data in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

Marketing and Advertising Cookies

Meet Cuba will only use marketing, advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies where their use is permitted by applicable law.

Such technologies may be capable of:

  • recognising visitors across sessions;
  • recording browsing activity;
  • measuring advertising activity;
  • building profiles based on interests or behaviour; or
  • enabling targeted communications.

Given the legal restrictions applicable to the advertising and promotion of tobacco products, we will not deploy cookies for tobacco-product advertising or promotional targeting unless we are satisfied that the proposed use is permitted by applicable law.

If marketing cookies involving personal data are used, any additional notices or consents required by applicable law will be provided.

8.  Managing Cookies Through Your Browser

Most web browsers allow you to manage cookies through their settings.

Learn more about managing cookies in your browser.⌄

Depending on your browser, you may be able to:

  • view stored cookies;
  • delete cookies;
  • block all cookies;
  • block third-party cookies;
  • restrict certain websites from setting cookies; or
  • receive notifications when cookies are being set.

Please refer to your browser's help or privacy settings for instructions.

Blocking or deleting cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

For example, you may be unable to:

  • keep products in your shopping bag;
  • remain signed into your account;
  • complete certain parts of checkout;
  • retain website preferences; or
  • use other functions that depend on cookies.
