1.  Hong Kong Delivery

We currently offer delivery within Hong Kong to eligible addresses.

Delivery fee: HK$100

Complimentary delivery: Orders over HK$2,000

Estimated delivery time: 1–3 business days

Available delivery options and any applicable charges will be displayed during checkout before you place your order.

Delivery times are estimates and may occasionally be affected by circumstances outside our reasonable control.

2.  Delivery Areas

We currently deliver across Hong Kong.

Learn more about where we deliver.⌄

We currently deliver to:

  • Hong Kong Island
  • Kowloon
  • New Territories

Certain locations may be excluded or subject to additional arrangements.

If we are unable to deliver to your address, our team will contact you after your order is placed.

3.  Order Processing

Orders are generally processed during our normal business hours.

Orders placed after 2:00 PM may be processed on the following business day.

Orders placed on public holidays or outside business hours may require additional processing time.

Rare, limited, pre-order or specially sourced products may require longer preparation periods.

Where this applies, we will provide relevant information on the product page or contact you after your order is placed.

4.  Order Confirmation

Once your order has been submitted, you will receive an order acknowledgement using the contact details provided at checkout.

This acknowledgement confirms that we have received your order. It does not necessarily mean that the order has been accepted.

Once your order has been confirmed and prepared for delivery, we will provide an update where applicable.

5.  Age Verification

Tobacco products must not be sold or supplied to persons under the age of 18.

Learn more about age verification at delivery.⌄

We may request satisfactory proof of age or identity before:

  • accepting an order;
  • dispatching an order;
  • completing delivery; or
  • releasing an order for collection.

The person receiving or collecting tobacco products must be legally permitted to receive them.

If satisfactory age verification cannot be completed, we may refuse delivery or collection and the order may be returned to us.

6.  Receiving Your Order

Please ensure that someone eligible to receive the order is available at the delivery address.

Learn more about what the courier may require.⌄

Depending on the delivery method, the courier may require:

  • a signature;
  • identification;
  • proof of age; or
  • other reasonable verification.

Tobacco products should not be left with a person under 18.

We may instruct our delivery provider not to leave tobacco products unattended.

7.  Failed Delivery

If delivery cannot be completed, the order may be returned to us and we will contact you to arrange the next step.

Learn more about why delivery may fail.⌄

Delivery cannot be completed where:

  • nobody eligible is available to receive the order;
  • age verification cannot be completed;
  • the address provided is incorrect or incomplete; or
  • access to the delivery location is unavailable.

An additional delivery charge may apply where a further delivery attempt is required due to circumstances within the customer's control.

8.  Incorrect Delivery Information

Please check your delivery information carefully before completing checkout.

If you notice an error after placing your order, contact us as soon as possible at:

Where available, you may choose to collect your order from our Hong Kong store.

10.  Inspecting Your Order

Please inspect your order as soon as reasonably possible after receiving or collecting it.

Learn more about what to do if something's wrong.⌄

If anything appears to be:

  • damaged;
  • missing;
  • incorrect; or
  • materially different from what you ordered,

please contact us promptly.

Where possible, please keep:

  • the product;
  • its original packaging;
  • the delivery packaging;
  • shipping labels; and
  • photographs showing the issue.

This will help us assess and resolve the matter efficiently.

11.  Returns

We want you to be satisfied that you have received the product you ordered. Eligible products may be returned within 14 days of delivery or collection.

Learn more about return eligibility.⌄

To be eligible for a discretionary return, a product must generally:

  • be unused;
  • be unopened;
  • remain in its original condition;
  • retain all original packaging, seals and labels; and
  • be accompanied by satisfactory proof of purchase.

Please contact us before returning any product.

Products sent back without prior authorisation may take longer to process.

12.  Tobacco Products

Because tobacco is a consumable product that is sensitive to handling and environmental conditions, we generally cannot accept a discretionary return once it has been opened or used.

Learn more about when a tobacco return isn't possible.⌄

We generally cannot accept a discretionary return once:

  • the product has been opened;
  • protective packaging or seals have been removed or damaged;
  • the cigars have been removed from their original packaging;
  • the product has been smoked or used;
  • the product has been improperly stored; or
  • its condition can no longer reasonably be verified.

This does not affect any rights or remedies available to you under applicable law where goods are damaged, defective, incorrect or otherwise not supplied as agreed.

13.  Natural Variation

Cigars are handmade agricultural products, so reasonable differences may occur between individual cigars and boxes.

Learn more about natural variation.⌄

Reasonable differences may occur between individual cigars, boxes and production batches, including variations in:

  • wrapper colour;
  • texture;
  • grain;
  • natural markings;
  • wooden packaging;
  • bands; and
  • presentation.

Minor natural variations of this kind are not normally considered defects.

14.  Damaged Products

If your order arrives materially damaged, please contact us as soon as reasonably possible.

Learn more about reporting damage.⌄

Please include:

  • your order number;
  • a description of the issue; and
  • clear photographs of the product and packaging.

Do not dispose of the product or its packaging until our team has advised you that it is appropriate to do so.

After reviewing the issue, we may arrange an appropriate remedy, which may include replacement, return or refund depending on the circumstances.

15.  Incorrect Products

If we have sent you an item different from the product shown on your order confirmation, please contact us promptly.

Learn more about what happens next.⌄

We may arrange for the incorrect item to be collected or returned and, where appropriate, provide:

  • the correct product;
  • a replacement; or
  • a refund.

Please keep the incorrect product unopened and in its original condition wherever reasonably possible.

16.  Missing Items

If part of your order is missing, please contact us and provide your order number.

We will review the order and delivery records and arrange an appropriate resolution where an item has not been supplied.

17.  Cancellations

If you wish to cancel an order, please contact us as soon as possible.

Orders that have not yet been dispatched may be cancelled where operationally possible.

Once an order has entered the delivery process, we may no longer be able to cancel it.

Any applicable return arrangements will then apply.

18.  Pre-Orders

Pre-order products are subject to their own availability and expected release dates.

Learn more about pre-order delays.⌄

Any expected delivery or availability date shown for a pre-order is an estimate unless expressly stated otherwise.

Delays may occur due to:

  • supplier allocation;
  • production;
  • international transport;
  • customs;
  • importation;
  • regulatory requirements; or
  • other circumstances outside our reasonable control.

If a significant delay affects your pre-order, we will contact you with the information reasonably available to us.

Specific cancellation or refund conditions may apply to certain pre-order or specially sourced products and will be disclosed where relevant.

19.  Limited and Special Editions

Rare, discontinued, allocated and limited-edition products may have additional return or cancellation restrictions.

Where special conditions apply, they will be stated before purchase or communicated to you when the order is confirmed.

This does not affect rights that cannot legally be excluded.

20.  Refunds

Once a refund has been approved, it will normally be made to the original payment method.

Please allow 5–10 business days for us to process an approved refund.

Your bank or payment provider may require additional time before the funds appear in your account.

We are not responsible for processing delays caused by your bank, card issuer or payment provider.

21.  Delivery Charges on Returns

Where a return is accepted because an item is damaged, defective or supplied incorrectly, we will deal with reasonable return-delivery costs in accordance with applicable law and the circumstances of the order.

For discretionary returns, the customer may be responsible for return-delivery costs.

Original delivery charges may not be refundable unless required by law or expressly agreed by us.

23.  Storage After Delivery

Once tobacco products have been delivered or collected, appropriate storage becomes the customer's responsibility.

Learn more about storing your cigars.⌄

Cigars may be adversely affected by inappropriate:

  • temperature;
  • humidity;
  • dryness;
  • heat;
  • sunlight; or
  • physical handling.

Damage or deterioration resulting from improper storage after delivery is not considered a delivery defect.

If you are unsure how to store your purchase, please contact our store for general guidance.
